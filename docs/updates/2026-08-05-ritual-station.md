# TattooMagic — Progress Update, August 5

## What we're building

TattooMagic is a RimWorld mod that turns tattoos into a real source of
power for your colonists. Instead of being purely cosmetic, a magic
tattoo is something a colonist earns and grows into: applied at a
dedicated ritual station, it can grant a passive buff (like extra armor
or resistance to fire) or a usable ability with its own cooldown (like a
short teleport or a battlefield taunt). There are 11 tattoos planned at
launch, each one gets stronger the more it's used, and colonists start
with room for one tattoo but can earn a couple more slots over time. The
mod is being built to work whether or not you're also running Combat
Extended.

## What we got working tonight

Tonight's focus was the ritual station itself — the actual workbench and
the process of applying a tattoo. In-game, you can now build the
station, pick a colonist to receive a tattoo and a colonist to perform
the ritual, and choose which tattoo to apply. The ritual consumes real
ingredients, and whether it succeeds depends on the performing
colonist's Artistic skill — a skilled artist will rarely botch it, while
an unskilled one risks wasting the materials on a failed attempt. We
tested this live in a fresh colony tonight and successfully applied a
tattoo to a colonist from start to finish, ingredients and all.

## What's not in yet

Right now, applying a tattoo works, but the tattoos themselves don't do
anything magical yet — that "grant the actual buff or ability" piece,
along with the system for earning extra tattoo slots, is the next chunk
of work. A few more test passes on edge cases (what happens if a ritual
is interrupted, running low on skill, etc.) are also still on the list
before this first piece is fully wrapped up.
