# TattooMagic — Progress Update, August 15

## What we're building

TattooMagic is a RimWorld mod that turns tattoos into a real source of power for your colonists. Instead of being purely cosmetic, a magic tattoo is something a colonist earns and grows into: applied at a dedicated ritual station, it can grant a passive buff (like extra armor or resistance to fire) or a usable ability with its own cooldown (like a short teleport or a battlefield taunt). There are 11 tattoos planned at launch, each one gets stronger the more it's used, and colonists start with room for one tattoo but can earn a couple more slots over time. The mod is being built to work whether or not you're also running Combat Extended.

## What we got working today

**Berserker's Mark** is live — the fifth and final ability-style tattoo, after Guardian's Call, Stormlash, Bloodrune, and Wraithstep. That rounds out the entire "activate it yourself" half of the tattoo roster; everything left to build from here is passive. Click it and the colonist trades defense for a burst of melee damage and staying power: for a short window, they hit harder in melee and can shrug off more pain before going down, but their armor drops at the same time — a real risk for a real reward, then a cooldown before it can be used again. Use it enough and it upgrades in place to a stronger version: an even bigger damage boost, and the defense penalty actually gets smaller (though never zero), so the trade-off keeps paying off the more a colonist leans on it.

Your artwork needed the same cleanup Bloodrune's and Wraithstep's did — background and an AI-generator watermark removed before it would sit right in the ability bar — but this time the icon still showed up broken afterward, as a plain red "not found" box. That one took real digging: the fixed artwork was correct and in the right place in our project, but the actual copy of the mod RimWorld loads from lives in a separate folder, and our build only refreshes that folder's images when we rebuild the code — which we'd last done before the new icon existed. Once we understood that, the fix was simple, but it's worth flagging because it's a gap in our own process, not something you did wrong: from now on, any time we hand you new artwork or data to test, we'll make sure a fresh build has actually happened first, rather than assuming last session's build still covers it.

The real trust-building moment today was the pain-threshold trade-off itself. Rather than take our word that it worked, you deliberately hurt a test colonist until her pain sat in exactly the dangerous zone — high enough that she'd normally be at risk of going down, but still safely under the tattoo's boosted threshold — activated Berserker's Mark, and confirmed she stayed on her feet. Then, critically, you let the boost run out *without* healing her, and watched her go down the instant it expired and her pain was still above her normal limit. That's not a bug — it's the intended cost of using the ability while already badly hurt — and seeing it happen on purpose, on command, is a much stronger confirmation than any of us just asserting the math is right. Along the way we also confirmed something reassuring: RimWorld's own armor-summary panel simply doesn't display this kind of temporary tattoo bonus at all (it never has, for any tattoo), so its not changing wasn't a sign of anything broken — we verified the defense penalty was applying correctly through a different, more direct check instead.

Last thing: we reused the direct-removal-call testing tool built for Bloodrune back on the 14th to confirm Berserker's Mark can't be stripped off through any back door either, and separately confirmed it coexists cleanly with Wraithstep on the same colonist — both abilities' cooldowns and progress stayed completely independent of each other, and using one didn't disturb the other at all.

## What's not in yet

4 of 11 tattoos are still inert — applying them works, but they don't grant anything yet, and all four of the ones left are passive buffs rather than click-to-activate abilities. Numbers are still placeholders across the board, same as every tattoo so far — Berserker's Mark's damage boost, pain-threshold increase, defense penalty, and how many uses it takes to reach its stronger tier are all deliberately small test values, not tuned for real balance yet.
