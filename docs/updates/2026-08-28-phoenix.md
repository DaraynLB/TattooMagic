# TattooMagic — Progress Update, August 28

## What we're building

TattooMagic is a RimWorld mod that turns tattoos into a real source of power for your colonists. Instead of being purely cosmetic, a magic tattoo is something a colonist earns and grows into: applied at a dedicated ritual station, it can grant a passive buff or a usable ability with its own cooldown. The original 11-tattoo launch roster wrapped up back in August, and since then we've been building something bigger and stranger than anything before it: **Phoenix**, a tattoo about literally cheating death.

## What we've tested so far

**Coming back from actual death.** We applied Phoenix to test colonists, killed them outright, and confirmed they genuinely come back to life on their own after a wait — with a nasty burn left behind every single time, whether the attempt succeeds or fails. We've now watched this happen for real, more than once, not just on paper.

**Deliberate cremation.** Choosing to cremate a Phoenix-tattooed colonist's body yourself, on purpose, guarantees their next attempt to come back succeeds — no matter how decomposed the body already was — at the cost of losing whatever gear was left on them. Confirmed working exactly as intended.

**The 3-tattoo colony limit.** This is meant to be a rare and powerful tattoo, so only 3 colonists can wear it across your whole colony at once. We tested this from every angle: trying to apply a 4th gets correctly blocked, a colonist who's dead and waiting to revive still counts against the limit, successfully coming back still counts, permanently losing a wearer frees up their spot again, and a new recruit who already happens to have the tattoo is allowed to keep it — temporarily pushing you over 3 — without that opening the door for anyone else to sneak in a 4th. We also confirmed a wearer who leaves your colony (captured, recruited away, etc.) properly loses the tattoo and frees their slot the instant it happens.

**The passive bleeding protection.** Separate from the death-and-revival mechanic entirely, this tattoo also gives a colonist a chance to automatically cauterize their worst bleeding wound before it becomes fatal. We confirmed it correctly ignores minor scratches, always prioritizes a severed limb over an ordinary wound even when the ordinary wound looks worse on paper, and never interferes with the revival timer or anything else the tattoo does.

## Issues we found (and fixed)

- A colonist who was permanently, unrecoverably lost was getting re-flagged over and over every few seconds — not something you'd ever notice in play, but it was cluttering the game's internal log for no reason. Fixed.
- Once a tattoo ritual was queued up for a colonist, there was no way to back out of it if you changed your mind and gave that colonist a different order — they'd stay stuck "waiting" forever with nothing actually happening. We added a proper cancel option so this can't happen anymore.
- The wait-before-reviving time couldn't be set to anything less than a full day, which would've made it awkward to fine-tune later. Fixed so it can be set to any amount of time, including part of a day.
- The bleeding-protection passive could throw an error in the specific case where it tried to cauterize a limb that had already been severed entirely. Fixed.
- One thing worth flagging as an intentional design choice, not a bug: coming back from death doesn't erase whatever originally killed the colonist. If the fatal blow was severe enough to leave lasting damage once healed — a badly damaged brain, say — that damage comes back with them. They're alive, but potentially much worse for wear. We think that's the right cost for cheating death, but wanted you to know it's deliberate before you run into it.

## What's left before this is ready

- One more piece of the bleeding-protection testing: confirming the upgraded version of the tattoo triggers noticeably more often than the base version, while the burn it leaves stays the same either way.
- Confirming the tattoo genuinely can't be removed through normal means — only through cheat-level tools — and that removing it that way correctly frees up a colony slot.
- A full save-and-reload check, making sure nothing about a pending revival, a guaranteed cremation, or the tattoo's upgraded tier gets lost or duplicated if you save and come back later.
- A final pass confirming Phoenix plays nicely with every other tattoo and with Combat Extended, and that it works fine even without the optional DLC installed.

Once that's done, the mechanics themselves are finished — but like the rest of the roster, everything is currently running on placeholder numbers so we could focus on proving the mechanics work correctly first. Tuning how long the wait actually is, how good the odds are, and so on for real balance still comes after that.
