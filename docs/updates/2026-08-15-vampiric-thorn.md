# TattooMagic — Progress Update, August 15 (Vampiric Thorn)

## What we're building

TattooMagic is a RimWorld mod that turns tattoos into a real source of power for your colonists. Instead of being purely cosmetic, a magic tattoo is something a colonist earns and grows into: applied at a dedicated ritual station, it can grant a passive buff (like extra armor or resistance to fire) or a usable ability with its own cooldown (like a short teleport or a battlefield taunt). There are 11 tattoos planned at launch, each one gets stronger the more it's used, and colonists start with room for one tattoo but can earn a couple more slots over time. The mod is being built to work whether or not you're also running Combat Extended.

## What we got working today

**Vampiric Thorn** is live — a passive tattoo that turns melee combat into self-healing. Every time a tattooed colonist lands a melee hit that actually deals damage, they recover a small amount of their own worst wound, whether they're brawling bare-fisted, swinging a knife, or fighting off a raider. Land enough hits and it upgrades in place to a stronger version: bigger healing per hit, and finishing off an enemy in melee grants a few extra seconds of continued recovery on top of the normal per-hit heal — a real payoff for pressing an advantage instead of disengaging.

Building this also meant extending the same shared "something hit something" plumbing that already powers Frost Sigil's and Serpent's Eye's effects, adding the missing piece for "this colonist's own melee attack just landed" — the same way we've extended it once before for ranged attacks. And since this is now the second tattoo that needs to heal a colonist's own wounds (Bloodrune, back on the 14th, was the first), we pulled that "heal the worst wound, then the next one" logic out into one shared, reusable piece both tattoos now use — so there's exactly one place that logic lives and gets tested, not two copies quietly drifting apart over time.

That consolidation is also where today's real story is. While testing Vampiric Thorn live — genuinely trying to break it, not just confirm the happy path — you hit an actual game freeze partway through a fight, bad enough that Windows offered to force-close the game. We tracked it down fast: the shared healing logic could get stuck in an infinite loop in one specific situation — a colonist's last open wound closing completely while there was still more healing "left in the tank" to apply. It's a real bug, and it would have shipped invisibly if you hadn't been pushing the effect hard enough, and long enough, to actually trigger it — most testing sessions wouldn't have run enough consecutive hits to fully close out every wound on a colonist mid-fight. We fixed it, rebuilt, and you then spent the rest of the session specifically re-creating that exact condition — wounds fully closing, mid-combat, mid-recovery-burst — and confirmed it holds clean now. That's exactly the kind of testing that catches the bugs a quick glance never would.

Beyond that, it was a genuinely thorough session: two colonists reached the upgraded tier and it kicked in exactly on schedule, two separate killing blows both correctly triggered the bonus recovery window (including one where a wound visibly finished closing entirely from that recovery burst alone, no further hits needed), misses and dodges confirmed to do nothing at all, a colonist getting hit by someone *else's* attack confirmed the tattoo doesn't react to that either, a save-and-reload picked up a colonist's progress exactly where it left off, and the existing anti-tampering protection (the same one that stops other mods from silently stripping a tattoo off) held for Vampiric Thorn specifically, while Developer Mode's own override still worked as expected.

## What's not in yet

3 of 11 tattoos are still inert — applying them works, but they don't grant anything yet, and all three of the ones left are passive buffs. Numbers are still placeholders across the board, same as every tattoo so far — Vampiric Thorn's healing-per-hit, bonus recovery amount, and how many hits it takes to reach its stronger tier are all deliberately small test values, not tuned for real balance yet.
