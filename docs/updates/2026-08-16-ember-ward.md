# TattooMagic — Progress Update, August 16

## What we're building

TattooMagic is a RimWorld mod that turns tattoos into a real source of power for your colonists. Instead of being purely cosmetic, a magic tattoo is something a colonist earns and grows into: applied at a dedicated ritual station, it can grant a passive buff (like extra armor or resistance to fire) or a usable ability with its own cooldown (like a short teleport or a battlefield taunt). There are 11 tattoos planned at launch, each one gets stronger the more it's used, and colonists start with room for one tattoo but can earn a couple more slots over time. The mod is being built to work whether or not you're also running Combat Extended.

## What we got working today

**Ember Ward** is live — a passive tattoo that makes a colonist harder to burn. While worn, it raises how much heat they can tolerate before it bothers them, and it quietly reduces the damage they take from fire. Get burned enough times and it upgrades in place to a stronger version: more heat tolerance, more damage reduction, and a real chance to shrug off a burn instance entirely and take nothing at all.

Your new artwork for it needed the same background cleanup as the earlier tattoos, and this time our own automated tool actually did a worse job than you did by hand — it inconsistently erased part of the flame effect on one side of the image. We swapped in your version instead, just trimmed the "made with AI" watermark off the corner to match how the other icons look.

The real story today was tracking down why the fire-damage reduction didn't seem to be working, and it took two separate rounds of digging to get to the bottom of it. The first time it looked broken, the cause turned out to be our own testing tool: RimWorld's built-in "deal some damage" debug button doesn't attribute the hit to anyone, and it turns out Combat Extended quietly skips its own armor math entirely whenever a hit has no identifiable source — so our test was accidentally proving nothing at all, in either direction. Once we understood that and switched to testing with an actual fire instead of the debug button, the numbers still didn't move. That second result was the real one, and it's where you made the call that mattered: rather than let us just try a bigger number and hope, you asked directly what a bigger number would even prove if the underlying problem was that the reduction wasn't being applied at all — and that question was exactly right. It sent us back into Combat Extended's own code instead of more guessing, where we found a genuine, narrow gap: its armor calculations only check a colonist's own built-in resistances (as opposed to what they're wearing) for creatures with natural hide or plating — never for ordinary humans — so the tattoo's contribution was invisible to that one calculation no matter how strong we made it. We fixed it with a direct, guaranteed-correct calculation that only runs when Combat Extended is loaded, sitting alongside the already-correct version used the rest of the time.

We confirmed the fix properly rather than taking the code's word for it: with an actual fire burning, a tattooed colonist took noticeably less damage than an untattooed one standing in the same fire — around 36% of the fire's damage got through with the tattoo on, versus about 56% with it off, a clean and repeatable gap in exactly the direction it should be. Along the way we also confirmed the tattoo's other pieces are unaffected — removing it stops every effect immediately, its progress survived several game restarts without resetting, our existing anti-tampering protection covers it the same as every other tattoo with no extra work needed, and it doesn't interfere with any of the other three combat tattoos already in the mod.

## What's not in yet

2 of 11 tattoos are still inert — applying them works, but they don't grant anything yet, and both of the ones left are passive buffs. Numbers are still placeholders across the board, same as every tattoo so far — Ember Ward's heat tolerance, damage reduction, and how many burns it takes to reach its stronger tier are all deliberately small test values, not tuned for real balance yet.
